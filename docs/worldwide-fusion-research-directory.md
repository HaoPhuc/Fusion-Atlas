# Worldwide nuclear fusion research directory

Compiled from sources accessed on 14 November 2026. Source dates vary and are identified below.

**277 entries identified in fusion research/development sources, 24 additional university leads, and 8 coordinating organizations.**

This is a broad, sourced working directory, not a complete census of every organization worldwide. It covers fusion experiments, theory, materials, fuel cycles, diagnostics, engineering, and commercial technology development. It excludes organizations identified only as investors, customers, generic suppliers, or unrelated plasma users. Some companies develop enabling technologies, neutron sources, or propulsion rather than electricity-generating reactors.

Evidence has different strengths: an institutional research page is stronger than a sector directory. Consortium or directory membership does not establish the size of a programme, current hiring, operating facilities, or independently validated performance. Older survey entries are retained for discovery and explicitly flagged; they are not claims of current operation.

Most institution names are consolidated at parent organization level. Named constituent institutes and separately managed laboratories are retained when useful, so the entry count is not a count of independent legal entities. PPPL is listed separately from Princeton University; facilities such as NIF are included under their host laboratories. Company headquarters were not systematically verified: “International roster” means the source is a worldwide list, not that the company has offices worldwide.

Coverage is strongest in Europe, North America, and East Asia. Africa, Latin America, smaller university groups, new companies, and enabling-technology suppliers remain incompletely covered. The additional university leads section is deliberately separate because its source includes general plasma research and education, not exclusively nuclear fusion research.

## Directories for extending the search

- [IAEA Fusion Facility Database](https://nucleus.iaea.org/sites/fusion-portal/SitePages/FFDB.aspx?web=1): find facilities, their host organizations, and recorded status.
- [ITER worldwide fusion links](https://www.iter.org/scientists/worldwide-fusion-links): public research institutions and university programmes; some links and names are historical.
- [EUROfusion member country pages](https://euro-fusion.org/eurofusion/members/): follow each national research unit to its affiliated universities and institutes.
- [Fusion Industry Association membership](https://www.fusionindustryassociation.org/about/members/): distinguishes fusion developers, suppliers, affiliates, and education/research partners.
- [University Fusion Association university list](https://sites.google.com/site/universityfusionassociation/education): useful US discovery list, but includes plasma research and educational programmes beyond fusion.

For a genuinely exhaustive census, define an activity window (for example, a fusion-specific publication, funded project, experiment, or technical development within the previous three years), then check every national consortium affiliate, facility host, corporate developer, and recent publication affiliation against that rule. This directory has not completed that institution-by-institution publication audit.
